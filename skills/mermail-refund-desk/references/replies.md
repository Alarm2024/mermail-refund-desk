# Reply drafts

Write the draft in the customer's language (the checker's `language`), keep it short, and fill in just what the email and the policy state. Never promise a date the shop does not control, never say the money was sent, and never repeat an address, card or link from the customer's email.

## acknowledge

> Thank you for writing. We have your refund request{{ for order ORDER}}.
>
> We match it to the purchase and send the money back to the card, PayPal account or wallet that paid — nowhere else. We will reply again when it is done. You do not need to give a reason.
>
> {{SHOP}}

## escalate — destination change

> Thank you for writing. We have your refund request{{ for order ORDER}}.
>
> Refunds go back to the card, PayPal account or wallet that paid, so we cannot send this one to a different address. If you paid from an exchange, please write to us from the email address you bought with and tell us so; we will check it with you before anything is sent.
>
> {{SHOP}}

## escalate — sender or purchase does not match

> Thank you for writing. We could not match this request to a purchase made from this email address. Please write to us from the address you used when you bought, with the order number if you have it.
>
> {{SHOP}}

## escalate — already refunded

> Thank you for writing. Our records show a refund for this purchase already. We are checking it and will reply to you directly.
>
> {{SHOP}}

## quarantine and skip

No draft. Quarantined emails are starred and filed for the owner to read.

## Languages

The shop's site speaks English, Arabic, Russian, Chinese, German and Spanish. Translate the template faithfully; do not add claims in translation. If the language is not one of the policy's `reply_languages`, draft in English.
